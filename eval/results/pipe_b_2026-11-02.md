## Finding

Pipe B is **perfect on this corpus and far cheaper**: 100% feasibility decisions, 100% valid plans,
and (unlike Pipe A `full`) it gets `fyp_and_urop` right and never violates the internship-occupancy
rule — the solver performs the CP→B&D allocation and enforces occupancy exactly. Translation is near
dictation on these auto-draft prompts (fidelity 35–36/36), so the pipeline's reliability is the
solver's. Cost: $0.02–0.28 total for 36 requests (3.6–10× cheaper per request than Pipe A `full`, warm
cache), because the spec is a short fixed-shape output (~260–780 tokens) versus a full plan plus
reasoning.
