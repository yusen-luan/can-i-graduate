## Headline finding (changed from the pre-fix corpus)

The realism corrections make this corpus **separate LLM-direct from the solver**, where the earlier
35-instance corpus had both pipelines at the 100% ceiling.

- **One shared decision error.** All three Pipe A `full` models call **`fyp_and_urop` infeasible**
  (it is feasible). They reproduce the exact pre-fix bug — treating the 12 MC CP cap as applying to
  every CP module instead of the B&D-*allocated* credit — that Issue #1 corrected in the solver.
- **Plan validity collapses to 2–3 / 18.** Although decisions are 97.2%, almost every direct plan
  violates the **internship-occupancy** rule (14–15 of 18): the model schedules a full coursework load
  in the same semester as a 6-month full-time internship. The solver (Pipe B) never does.
- Model size barely matters: Haiku/Sonnet/Opus behave almost identically within the pipeline.
