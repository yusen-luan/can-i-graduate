## Notes
- UNSAT reasons were spot-checked and name the true binding rule (e.g. T2: 4-MC granularity caps
  6×27 MC at 146 MC of load; FYP+UROP: CP4101 alone fills the 12 MC CP cap). Not lucky guesses.
- Haiku thinks longest (mean ~8.9k output tokens vs Sonnet ~2.0k, Opus ~1.1k) and so is slowest.
- **Superseded first run** (`v1_promptbug/`, runs in `eval/runs/pipe_a_v1_promptbug/`): the catalog
  tagged CP3106/CP3209/CP4101/CP4106 "industrial experience" (their data pillar), contradicting the
  rule "no industrial-experience module outside the chosen option" (the checker uses the option sets).
  Haiku 34/35 (wrongly UNSAT on `internship_and_urop`, citing exactly that tag); Opus 16/17 valid
  (`no_h2_math`: CS1231S in sem 1 alongside its prereq MA1301). Fixed in `llm/prompts.py`, all rerun.
- Implication: with every rule stated explicitly and auto-draft prompts that spell out each knob,
  this corpus does not separate LLM-direct from the solver. The report's §7.2 predicted failure modes
  (T2 over-prediction, zero-slack mis-placements) did not occur at n=1 per model.
