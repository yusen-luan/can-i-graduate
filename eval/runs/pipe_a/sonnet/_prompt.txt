# 1. Planning model
- The plan has semesters numbered 1..T, where T is the number of semesters the student wants to graduate within. Never use a semester number outside 1..T.
- By default plan semester s is NUS Semester 1 when s is odd and NUS Semester 2 when s is even.
- If the student designates some plan semesters as Special Terms, the remaining (regular) semesters alternate Sem 1, Sem 2, Sem 1, ... in order starting with Sem 1, skipping the special slots. A special slot that follows a Sem 2 is Special Term 3; one that follows a Special Term 3 is Special Term 4.
- A module may only be placed in a semester whose term is listed in its "offered" terms.
- Each module is taken at most once. Never schedule a module the student has already completed or been exempted from.
- Prerequisites: every prerequisite of a module must be satisfied by a module completed/exempted before planning or scheduled in a STRICTLY EARLIER semester (not the same one). Unless the student says otherwise, assume A-level H2 Mathematics (so MA1301, MA1301X count as satisfied as prerequisites and are not taken) and direct English placement (so ES1000, ES1103 count as satisfied and are not taken). A student without H2 Mathematics, or with an English bridging requirement, must schedule those bridging modules like any other.
- Unit load: the units in a semester may not exceed the student's per-semester cap (or a semester-specific cap they give; "taking a semester off" means nothing at all may be placed in it), and never exceed 28 MC. Industrial-experience modules (CP3107, CP3110, CP3200, CP3202, CP3880, ETP3201L, ETP3205, IS4010) do NOT count toward a semester's unit load (they run over the vacation), but they still occupy a semester, must respect its offered terms, and cannot be placed in a semester taken off.
- CS2101, CS2103T must be taken together in the same semester.
- Year-long service learning: if you take one of these, its continuation must be in the immediately following plan semester:
  - GEN2050X (Sem 1 half, carries the credit) then GEN2050Y (0 MC continuation)
  - GEN2070X (Sem 1 half, carries the credit) then GEN2070Y (0 MC continuation)
- Preclusions: at most one module of each group below may be held (completed or scheduled; exempted modules do not count):
